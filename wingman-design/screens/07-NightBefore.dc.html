The Night Before

Three things tonight so tomorrow you only have to get dressed.

  1. 1
    Trim Tonight, Shave Tomorrow Tidy the neckline and sideburns tonight so any redness settles. Shave in the morning, with the grain.
  2. 2
    Steam the Blazer Hang it in the bathroom during a hot shower and let it dry overnight. Creases at the elbows drop out without an iron.
  3. 3
    Lay It All Out Brush the suede loafers, thread the belt and set out the pocket square. Five fewer decisions at 6pm.

General grooming tips, not medical or skincare advice. Patch-test any new product first.
